🎯 What Is hf-dataset-survey?

hf-dataset-survey is a powerful yet simple tool that helps you find computer vision datasets without the technical headache. Imagine having a personal assistant that scans the entire Hugging Face platform for datasets, removes duplicates, looks at the images to check quality, and downloads everything you need - all automatically.

Whether you're a student, researcher, hobbyist, or just curious about AI, this tool saves you hours of tedious searching and manual checking. It uses smart Vision-Language Models (VLMs) to actually "look" at dataset images and tell you what's inside - so you know exactly what you're getting before you commit.

✨ Key Features

🔎 Auto Discovery

Scan hundreds of datasets automatically. No more manual searching through endless list of files and metadata.

🔄 Smart Deduplication

Automatically identifies and removes duplicate datasets, saving your precious storage space and time.

👁️ Visual Inspection

Uses Qwen-VL Vision-Language Model to "look" at images and verify quality - catches errors, corrupt files, or mismatched data.

📥 One-Click Download

Download the approved datasets directly to your computer. Clean, organized folders ready for your project.

⚙️ How It Works

  1. Discover - The tool scans Hugging Face for all available vision-related datasets.
  2. Deduplicate - It compares metadata and content to remove redundant entries.
  3. Inspect - Using VLMs, it analyzes sample images to verify quality and relevance.
  4. Download - You select what passes inspection, and everything downloads automatically.

🚀 Quick Start Guide

Once you've downloaded the software, follow these simple steps:

  1. Open the application by double-clicking the file.
  2. Click "Start Survey" to begin scanning.
  3. Review the results - the tool shows you previews.
  4. Select datasets you want by ticking the checkboxes.
  5. Click "Download Selected" and wait for everything to finish.
  6. Your datasets are now in a folder called "datasets" - ready to use!
